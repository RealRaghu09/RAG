FAISS is a library for efficient similarity search and clustering of dense vectors. Meta developed FAISS to support large-scale nearest neighbor problems in research and production.

FAISS provides CPU and GPU implementations, product quantization, and several index types that trade accuracy for speed and memory. IndexFlatIP uses inner product; when embeddings are L2-normalized, cosine similarity is equivalent to inner product.

When building a RAG pipeline, practitioners often embed documents offline, add vectors to a FAISS index, serialize the index to disk, and load it at query time for low-latency retrieval.
