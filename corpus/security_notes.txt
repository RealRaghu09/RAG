RAG systems must treat retrieved text as untrusted data. Attackers can poison corpora with instructions that try to override the system prompt. Always separate system instructions from user content and retrieved chunks in your message layout.

Log and monitor unusual retrieval patterns. Rate-limit embedding and search endpoints. Validate that answers cite chunks that were actually returned by the retriever.
