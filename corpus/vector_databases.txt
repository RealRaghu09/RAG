Vector databases are purpose-built systems for storing and retrieving vector embeddings using similarity search. They index high-dimensional vectors so applications can find nearest neighbors quickly at scale.

Retrieval-augmented generation, often called RAG, combines a language model with a retrieval step over a knowledge base. The retriever fetches relevant passages or chunks, and the model conditions its answer on that context instead of relying on parametric memory alone.

Pinecone is a managed vector database service. Teams use Pinecone when they want hosted infrastructure for approximate nearest neighbor search, metadata filtering, and operational simplicity without running their own ANN engine.

Other popular options include Milvus, Weaviate, Qdrant, and Chroma. Each differs in deployment model, filtering capabilities, and how embeddings are stored and updated.
